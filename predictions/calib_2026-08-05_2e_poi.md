## 🚨 Result 1 — the Holystrike residual is GONE

| Ability | school | weapon share | logged / modelled |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lightbound Cleave** (907304) | Holystrike | 86% | **1.00×** |
| **Dawnreaver** (903158) | Holystrike | 100% | **0.99×** |
| **Dawn Strike** (907901) | Holystrike | 85% | 0.79× |

`2c` reported a **~1.37×** Holystrike residual and planned a session around it.
With a same-session weapon input it is **1.00 and 0.99** — essentially exact.

✅ **This closes `holy_holystrike_ratio_weapon_input_confound`.** `2c` diagnosed
the cause correctly (a weapon number taken from a *different* session's parse
passes into a weapon-dominated ability at ~1:1) but could not demonstrate it
without a matching stat block. It is now demonstrated: **the entire Holystrike
residual was the stale weapon input.**

## 🚨 Result 2 — the Holy residual is TWO clusters, not one multiplier

| Ability | logged / modelled | has an SP/AP coefficient? |
|---|---|---|
| Righteous Smite (273123) | **2.27×** | ❌ none |
| Consecration (270768) | **2.20×** | ❌ none |
| Hammer from the Heavens (282987) | **1.45×** | ✅ 9.1% SP + 9.1% AP (hand-seeded) |
| Hour of Judgement (282984) | **1.41×** | ✅ inherits via the trigger chain |

**The split is not by school — it is by whether the spell has a coefficient at
all.** That is a mechanism, not a fitted constant:

- **The 2.2× pair are out-of-catalog spells** (Purification By Light's own
  Righteous Smite and Consecration). Session `2e` decoded their **flats** for the
  first time via the new `dbc_only` pass — but Ascension keeps applied
  coefficients in **tooltip text**, and an out-of-catalog spell has no catalog
  tooltip, so there is no `spell_scaling` row to carry one. Their SP term is
  **missing, not zero**. Back-solving gives ~0.37 SP for Righteous Smite and
  ~0.16 SP for Consecration — *stated as the size of the gap, deliberately NOT
  seeded*, because fitting a coefficient to the parse it is meant to check is
  precisely what `PHASE_2` T8 forbids.
- **The 1.41–1.45 pair do carry coefficients**, so their residual is the real
  remaining unmodelled term. Against the ×1.155 talent layer the calibrator
  excludes by design, ~1.43 / 1.155 = **~1.24 still unexplained**.

🛑 **Do not collapse these into a single "Holy amplifier".** Two different causes
that happen to land in one school would be averaged into a number that is wrong
for both.

## Result 3 — one ability is modelled TOO HIGH

**Sword Specialization (16459): 0.60×** — modelled ~782, logged 469.2. Its
decoded effect is type 31 (`weapon_damage_noschool`) with a flat 120, so the
model gives it *weapon + 120*. It is an **extra-attack proc**: a free swing.
A normal white swing in the same log averages 305.4 *including glancing*, and an
extra attack cannot glance — 469 is what an unglanced swing looks like.
**Hypothesis: the flat 120 does not apply, or applies to a different rank.**
Recorded, not patched.

## Gate outcome

Against `predictions/CALIBRATION_TOLERANCE.md` (±25% per ability):
**3 of 8 within tolerance** — but the composition matters far more than the
count. Every **weapon-damage** ability is now within ±1%; every miss is a Holy
ability, and each miss has a named mechanism rather than an unexplained gap.

`2c` reported **1 of 7**. The improvement came from data and provenance, not from
tuning: a same-session stat block, and the `dbc_only` decode that gave four of
these abilities a magnitude at all.

## Method note worth keeping

The residual **moved** between the unbuffed and buffed windows (HftH 1.446 →
1.577 by hand before the resolver ran), which per `NEXT_CAPTURE.md`'s own
decision table means part of it is buff state and part is structural. The
structural part survives in a window with **zero buffs and every input
measured** — which is the whole reason Window A had to be captured with the
imbue off.
