### F7 ⚠ `pair_parses_to_stats.py` hardcodes the crash gap at whole seconds; the bytes give 59.76 s — so "logged 319.8 s" silently disagrees with `3m`'s published (and this audit's re-measured) 320.1. CONFIRMED

`CRASH_GAP_LOCAL = ("19:57:30", "19:58:30")` → the tool subtracts 60.0 s. The two log
files' own stamps are `19:57:30.470` → `19:58:30.234`: **59.76 s**. Gehennas logged is
therefore ~320.0, not 319.8 — and `3m`/`AUDIT_3M` published **320.1** from the same
bytes. The session adopted 319.8 without noting the change. 0.08% today; but this tool
is the **denominator instrument for every future per-parse coefficient**, its comment
claims the gap was "measured … from the two log files' own stamps", and truncation is a
systematic bias in the direction that inflates DPS. Fix: derive the gap from the files'
first/last event stamps with milliseconds, not a hand-typed second-resolution constant.
