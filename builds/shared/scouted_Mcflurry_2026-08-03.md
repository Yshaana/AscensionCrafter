## Key insight
Zero crit investment is not a mistake — it's the build. Spirit is stacked
to 195 (above Intellect at 151), which only pays off via **Spiritual
Guidance** converting Spirit directly into Spell Power. Combined with a
kit that's almost entirely DoT/periodic damage (Shadow Word: Pain,
Devouring Plague, Lichplague, Mutation, Plague Swarm), this is a clean
**field confirmation of our own primer rule that periodic/ground effects
generally cannot crit** (primer §1) — if nearly all damage is ticking,
crit rating is dead itemization, so every point goes into Spirit->SP
conversion, Hit, and unusually heavy Spell Penetration (97, vs. Shadow
damage being resistible unlike Holy). Also far more consistent across
bosses than David's build (38-54k DPS band vs. David's spikier pattern).
