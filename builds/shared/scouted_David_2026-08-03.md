## Key insight
Nearly every ability-bar slot is a damage multiplier, not a new button —
Ruin/Unholy Power/Demonic Tactics/Master Demonologist all sit at full
rank. Gear is mediocre (8.7% hit, 7.93% crit, nowhere near capped) but
the DPS holds up almost entirely on talent stacking, not itemization.
Decimation's execute-phase cast-speed collapse explains why short ZG
fights (most under a minute) skew his peak numbers upward — worth
discounting outlier peaks on short pulls for this reason.
